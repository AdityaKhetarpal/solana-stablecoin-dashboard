TL;DR · Jul 1 – Sep 30, 2026
Q3 was the first quarter of 2026 where everything grew at once: SOL +62%, DeFi TVL +33%, stablecoin supply +5.1% - and Solana took back the #3 spot.
  • 01Supply and rank: Supply rose from $14.60B to $15.34B (+5.1%), with a 2026 high of $16.09B on July 25. Solana finished $0.96B ahead of BSC to reclaim #3 behind Ethereum and Tron.
  • 02Diversification continued: USDC's share fell to 47.0%. USD1 grew 36% to $1.39B, USDG/USDG0 grew 21% to $1.83B, and non-USDC/USDT supply passed $5.4B.
  • 03Activity surge: SOL rallied from $74 to $119, TVL climbed to $6.46B, and September brought 557M stablecoin transfers (+33% MoM) and 888K daily active stablecoin addresses.
01 - Supply

$15.34B and a new 2026 high

Supply jumped ~$2B in ten days to a $16.09B high (Jul 25), consolidated around $15B through August, and closed the quarter at $15.34B (+5.1% QoQ).

02 - Composition

USD1 is the quarter's breakout

USD1 added $370M (+36%) to reach $1.39B. USDG/USDG0 reached $1.83B and SoFiUSD appeared on DefiLlama at $235M - while USDC slipped to 47.0% of supply.

03 - Coupling

Supply moved with SOL - a little

The daily SOL-supply correlation rose to 0.24 from 0.02 in Q2 (0.03 in Q1). Still weak: most of the dollar base is payment and settlement demand, not SOL beta.

Daily active stablecoin addresses on Solana reached 888K in September, up from 333K a year earlier - the first time the figure crossed that level.

Solana carries 76% of agent-payment transactions
Solana processed 23.2M x402 (machine-to-machine) payment transactions in four weeks - an early stablecoin use case unique to low-fee chains.
Key takeaway
Q3 added bank rails and AI-agent payments to the card-network adoption from Q2. These flows are sticky and price-insensitive, which is why supply stayed firm even before SOL rallied.
Regulatory & Market
MiCA in Force, GENIUS Act Rules, Tokenized Markets
Key developments Q3 2026
i
Regulatory context from Stablecoin Insider and the Solana Foundation; global supply from DefiLlama.
MiCA (EU) · JUL 1
Enforcement begins - compliant issuers gain
MiCA enforcement from July 1 removed USDT from major EU venues. On Solana, newer regulated issuers (USDG, USD1, SoFiUSD) more than accounted for Q3's net growth.
SEC · SEP 17
Five-year exemption for tokenized NMS stocks
The SEC issued a five-year conditional exemption for qualifying tokenized-stock activity, a tailwind for stablecoin-settled equity trading where Solana leads.
Key takeaway
Global supply was flat in Q3 ($303.4B → $305.8B), yet Solana's share rose to 5.0%. Solana is gaining share of a market that has stopped growing - the clearest sign its demand is structural.
