TL;DR · Apr 1 – Jun 30, 2026
Q2 was a stress test: SOL fell another 10% and DeFi TVL dropped 20% - yet Solana's stablecoin base still grew, and the mix behind it changed faster than ever.
  • 01Supply held up: Total stablecoin supply rose 2.4% from $14.26B to $14.60B, with an intra-quarter high of $15.41B on May 15 - while SOL ended the quarter 9.5% lower.
  • 02USDC dominance cracked: USDC's share fell from 55.0% to 50.1% as $524M left the chain. USDG (incl. USDG0) grew 52% to $1.51B and is now the #3 Solana stablecoin; USDe arrived with $543M.
  • 03Rank race: BSC ($14.66B) edged Solana ($14.60B) by just $60M at quarter-end. Daily SOL-price and supply moves were essentially uncorrelated (r = 0.02).
01 - Supply

+2.4% in a down quarter

Supply touched $15.41B in mid-May, gave back gains in late May, then recovered through June to close at $14.60B - holding up while the rest of Solana DeFi shrank.

02 - Composition

USDC slips to a coin-flip majority

USDC fell to 50.1% of supply. Non-USDC/USDT stablecoins grew from $3.52B to $4.78B in one quarter, led by USDG/USDG0, USDe and USD1.

03 - Resilience

Stablecoins ignored SOL again

SOL swung between $97 and $62 and TVL fell 20%, but supply finished higher. The correlation of daily changes was 0.02 - effectively zero.

Institutional Adoption
Payment Rails & New Issuers
Q2 2026 highlights
i
Institutional developments from Q2 2026 reported by TokenPost, Solana Compass and Whale Alert.
MASTERCARD · JUN 3
Solana picked for a 24/7 stablecoin payments network
Mastercard named eight blockchains, including Solana, for a global stablecoin settlement network designed to run around the clock.
CIRCLE · JUN 8
$500M USDC minted - Solana passes 10% of global USDC
Two tranches totalling $500M pushed Solana above 10% of global USDC supply, a share that held through the rest of June.
SOFI BANK · APR–JUN
Bank-issued SoFiUSD launches on Solana
SoFiUSD went live on Solana in early April and passed $200M on-chain by June 30 (Artemis), with essentially all of its growth on Solana.
Key takeaway
Q2's pattern: card networks and banks choosing Solana as a settlement rail. That is new issuance and payments demand, which helps explain why supply grew while trading activity shrank.
Regulatory & Market
GENIUS Act, MiCA and a Softer Global Market
Key developments Q2 2026
i
Global supply from DefiLlama; regulatory context from Stablecoin Insider and Crystal Intelligence reporting on Q2 2026.
GLOBAL MARKET
Global supply peaked at $314B, then fell to $303B
Global stablecoin supply hit $314.4B on May 17 before an ~$11B pullback by June 30. Solana's share of global supply still edged up from 4.6% to 4.8%.
MiCA (EU) · JUL 1 DEADLINE
USDT squeezed out of regulated EU venues
The run-up to MiCA's July 1 enforcement deadline pushed EU platforms to delist USDT, favouring compliant issuers such as USDC, USDG and EURC.
Key takeaway
Regulation is rewarding compliant, reserve-backed issuers. On Solana that shows up as USDG and USD1 taking share while USDT shrank 13% in the quarter.
